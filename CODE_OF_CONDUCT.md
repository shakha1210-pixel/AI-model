# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Apeiron project a respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and open to different perspectives.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's credentials, private data,
  or confidential information.
- Help keep discussions accessible, relevant, and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and deliberate
disruption of project discussions are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainers through the repository's private vulnerability reporting
flow for security issues. For other conduct concerns, use the repository's
available private contact channel. Do not include secrets or personal data in
public reports.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
code reviews, and other official community channels.
